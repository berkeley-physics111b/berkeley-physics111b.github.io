LTC-SERIES: LTC-11, LTC-21 and LTC-10G

Cryogenic Temperature Controllers

The LTC-11, LTC-21 and LTC-10G are high performance cryogenic temperature controllers designed with the features required for exact measurements and control, but with ease of use in mind. Below 10K, temperature resolution and control of the Model LTC-21 is 0.1mK. The LTC-11 and LTC-21 utilize all most commonly used sensors supporting temperatures from 800K to the mK regime (LTC-21). Another outstanding feature is their control of temperature through an enhanced digital autotuning PID control algorithm. Other standard features include Datalogging, High & Low Alarm Set points, Dual Relay Outputs, IEEE/RS232 control, CE certification and NIST tracability. For further information and pricing on the LTC-11, LTC-21 and LTC-10G Temperature Controllers, please contact us at CRYO-TECHNICS.
